Cancellation & Refund Policy

EFFECTIVE DATE: OCTOBER 4, 2026

TRANSPARENT & FAIR BILLING: Try Pro free for 5 days. The mandate is set up on day one, the first monthly charge is after the trial, and you can cancel before then.

At Ledgerly, customer trust is our paramount objective. We maintain a fair, transparent cancellation and refund process for all subscribed users.

1. 1-Click Subscription Cancellation

  • Self-Service Cancellation: You can cancel your monthly subscription at any time directly through Settings > Manage Subscription inside the app or within your UPI application (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM).
  • Zero Future Debits: Upon cancellation, the recurring mandate terminates immediately, guaranteeing zero unwanted future charges.
  • Trial Cancellation: Cancelling during the five-day trial prevents the first ₹99 monthly charge.
  • After Billing: Cancelling after a monthly payment stops later recurring charges. Existing records remain available in read-only mode.

2. Refund Guidelines

  • Duplicate Charges: In the rare event of accidental double charges caused by banking or network discrepancies, a 100% full refund of ₹99 is processed immediately upon verification.
  • Voluntary Cancellations: Because Pro can be tried for five days before the first monthly charge, completed monthly charges are generally non-refundable.

3. Settlement & Banking Timelines

Whenever a duplicate transaction refund is initiated through the app via Razorpay, funds typically credit to your bank account or UPI handle within 5 to 7 business days depending on standard banking clearing cycles.

4. Inquiries Regarding Billing

For questions concerning refunds, please reach out via our Contact Page.
