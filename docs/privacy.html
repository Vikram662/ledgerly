Privacy Policy

EFFECTIVE DATE: OCTOBER 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy outlines how Ledgerly ("we", "our", or "us") manages and protects your personal and business records. We are committed to transparency and data sovereignty for civil contractors, shopkeepers, and construction supervisors.

1. Local-First Architecture

Ledgerly operates on a strict local-first data model. All operational records, including worker registries, daily attendance logs, project expenses, and material receipts, are stored locally on your device in an encrypted SQLite database. We do not host or operate central servers holding your construction ledger data.

2. Encrypted Google Drive Backup

When you enable the Google Drive backup integration:

  • The application utilizes the restricted drive.file OAuth permission scope, ensuring access only to files created by Ledgerly.
  • Backup databases and photograph attachments are encrypted on your device using AES-256 standards prior to upload.
  • We never access, view, or transmit your Google credentials or any unrelated documents in your personal Google Drive.

3. Payment Processing via Razorpay

Subscription payments for Ledgerly Pro (₹99/month) are handled through Razorpay Software Private Limited:

  • Payment information is processed directly by Razorpay under RBI guidelines and PCI-DSS compliance standards.
  • We do not record, view, or retain your credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, or NetBanking authentication credentials.

4. Camera & Storage Permissions

Camera and file storage permissions are requested solely for legitimate operational purposes, such as capturing receipts, challan photos, and exporting financial PDF reports. The app never accesses personal media libraries without explicit user consent.

5. Zero Third-Party Data Sharing

We do not monetize, sell, lease, or share your financial data, vendor contacts, or site information with advertising networks or data brokers.

6. Privacy Contact & Inquiries

For any questions or data rights requests, please contact our team at thekedaar.support@gmail.com or visit our Contact Page.
